# Ideal Pathway Stages

| # | Stage | Meaning | Who | Activities in data |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Registration | Patient ka record banta hai aur basic details li jaati hain | Front desk / reception staff | Registration |
| 2 | Triage | Severity check hoti hai aur priority decide hoti hai | Triage nurse | Triage |
| 3 | Tests | Triage ke baad X-Ray / imaging hoti hai taaki doctor ke paas report ready ho | Lab technician / radiology | X-Ray |
| 4 | Consultation | Doctor patient ko dekhta hai aur diagnosis / plan banata hai | Doctor | Doctor Consultation |
| 5 | Treatment | Medicine ya procedure di jaati hai | Doctor / nurse | - |
| 6 | Discharge | Patient ko summary ke saath ghar bheja jaata hai | Doctor / ward staff | Discharge |

## Unmatched activities (kisi stage se match nahi hui)

- None, sab activities match ho gayi
