# Required vs Optional Steps

| Step | Type | Clinical reason |
|---|---|---|
| Registration | Required | Bina registration ke patient ka record aur billing nahi ban sakta |
| Triage | Required | Priority aur severity pata karna safety ke liye zaroori hai (emergency mein skip ho sakta hai) |
| Consultation | Required | Diagnosis aur treatment ka decision doctor hi leta hai |
| Discharge | Required | Visit officially close hone ke liye discharge zaroori hai |
| Tests | Optional | Sirf tab hote hain jab doctor ne manga ho |
| Treatment | Optional | Kuch patients ko sirf advice milti hai, procedure ya medicine nahi |
